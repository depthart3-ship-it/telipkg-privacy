# Privacy Policy for TeliPKG

Last updated: October 15, 2026

TeliPKG ("the App") is developed by Shabih ul Hasan (Shb hasan) ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

## 1. About the App
TeliPKG provides information about mobile network packages (including low-cost packages), helpful phone codes, and a shortcut to official SIM information websites.

## 2. Information We Collect
We do not require you to create an account or log in. The App does not collect, store, or transmit your name, phone number, email address, CNIC number, or any other personal information.

## 3. SIM Information and CNIC
The App has a button that opens an official SIM information website. That website is operated by a third party and not by us. If you choose to enter your CNIC there, you do so on that website only. We never see, collect, or store your CNIC. Please read the privacy policy of that website before using it.

## 4. Advertising (Google AdMob)
The App shows ads using Google AdMob, a third-party service. AdMob may collect and use certain information to show and measure ads, such as:
- Advertising ID
- IP address
- Device information (model, operating system version)
- App usage and ad interaction data

This data is handled by Google under its own privacy policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can reset your Advertising ID or turn off ad personalization in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

## 5. How Information Is Used
Information collected by AdMob is used only to show ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud. We do not sell your personal information.

## 6. Data Sharing
We do not share your personal information with anyone, except Google AdMob as described above, or when the law requires it.

## 7. Third-Party Links and Services
The App may open external websites (such as official SIM information pages or mobile network websites). We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

## 8. Disclaimer
TeliPKG is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to any mobile network company or government authority. Package prices, codes, and details may change. Please confirm them with your mobile network before use.

## 9. Data Security
We take reasonable steps to protect the App and its users. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

## 10. Children's Privacy
The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 11. Your Rights
We do not store your personal data on our servers. To ask about, or request deletion of, any data related to you, contact us at shabihkhan779@gmail.com and we will respond as soon as possible.
